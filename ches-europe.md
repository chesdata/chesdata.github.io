<section>
  <div class="wrap split">
    <div><p class="label">About the series</p></div>
    <div class="prose">
      <p>Questions on parties' general position on European integration, several EU policies, general left/right, economic left/right, and social left/right are common to all surveys. More recent surveys also contain questions on non-EU policy issues, such as anti-elite rhetoric, immigration, redistribution, decentralization, and environmental policy. More information about the survey and the trend file can be found in the codebook.</p>

      <p>The number of countries increased from 14 Western European countries in 1999 to 24 current or prospective EU members in 2006 to 31 countries in 2024. In this time, the number of national parties grew from 143 to 279. The 1999&ndash;2024 trend survey includes all EU member states except Luxembourg, plus the United Kingdom.</p>

      <p>The data sets adopt the country code and party id schema of the CHES trend file, with extensions for newly covered parties, so new waves can be merged with the trend file.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
